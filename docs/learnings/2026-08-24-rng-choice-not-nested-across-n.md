fact: `np.random.default_rng(seed).choice(N, size=n, replace=False)` does NOT produce nested subsamples across different `n` at the same seed. A 50-example smoke run and a 500-example full run drawn this way share only 9 examples, not 50 — they are two largely independent resamples, not "the same subsample with more data added". This silently invalidates the natural reading of a smoke-vs-full comparison: when the smoke said mapped-k8 acc_norm 0.160 (8/50) and the full run said 0.312 (156/500), that is not one estimate refining another on shared items, and a two-sample test gives p ~= 0.025 (Fisher exact 0.024; two-proportion z = 2.24, p = 0.0251) — outside the conventional 0.05 band, so "within sampling noise" is the wrong characterisation. Fix for future runs: draw the largest sample once and take the smoke set as its prefix, so the sets are genuinely nested.
